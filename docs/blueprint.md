# **App Name**: GearVibe

## Core Features:

- Dynamic Product Catalog: Display a comprehensive catalog of tech gadgets, camera accessories, and music pedals with essential details like name, price, and category. Products will be dynamically loaded.
- Interactive 3D Product Previews: Implement simulated 3D visualizations for each product, featuring floating animations, slow automatic rotation, subtle vertical movement, and user interaction for rotation via swipe and light zoom on touch/hover.
- Mobile-First Hero Carousel: A responsive, autoplaying hero banner with video/image slides, textual overlays, and navigational dots, optimized for horizontal swiping on mobile devices to showcase key products and promotions.
- Dedicated Product Detail View (Modal): Present comprehensive information for individual products within a smooth modal or dedicated page, including compatibility badges, and direct call-to-action buttons for WhatsApp purchase.
- AI-Powered Solution Finder Tool: An AI tool that suggests relevant products to users based on common problems they describe, such as 'vibration in recording' or 'bad angles,' aligning with the 'problema / solución' section.
- WhatsApp Direct Purchase & Support: Integrate direct links and a floating button for WhatsApp to streamline product purchasing and provide immediate customer support, driving conversion as a primary communication channel.
- Categorized Product Browsing & Filters: Enable users to efficiently explore the product range through clearly defined categories (e.g., 'Selfie Sticks', 'Soportes para moto', 'Música / Audio') and simple filtering options.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary brand color: A vibrant, energetic yellow (#FFD200) inspired by a premium tech aesthetic, utilized prominently for calls to action, interactive elements, and key highlights.
- Secondary brand color: A slightly darker yellow (#FFB800) used to provide clear visual feedback for hover states and secondary interactive elements, maintaining consistency with the primary yellow.
- Neutral background: A clean, very light grey (#F5F5F5) serving as a minimalist and premium canvas that allows products and content to stand out with excellent contrast.
- Card and surface elements: Pure white (#FFFFFF) ensures a crisp, premium feel for product displays, information panels, and UI components, maintaining a clean visual hierarchy.
- Primary text color: Solid black (#000000) for excellent readability, providing a strong and modern presence against light backgrounds for all main textual content.
- Secondary text color: A subtle dark grey (#5F6368) used for less prominent information such as descriptions, maintaining content hierarchy without visually cluttering the interface.
- Delicate borders: A very light grey (#E5E5E5) utilized for subtle divisions, creating clean visual structure and enhancing the overall minimalist aesthetic.
- Primary font: 'Inter' (sans-serif) for all text elements. Its modern, highly readable, and versatile design ensures a clear, objective feel, consistent with the tech and minimalist aesthetic. Note: currently only Google Fonts are supported.
- Utilize clean, modern line-art icons that are visually minimal yet descriptive, ensuring they complement the tech-inspired, uncluttered interface. Favor scalable SVG formats for optimal rendering.
- Mobile-first responsive design, featuring fluid grids and adaptive components to ensure an optimal experience across all screen sizes. Key interactive elements, such as 'pill-shaped' buttons, are prominently designed for ease of use.
- The homepage follows a structured flow: an immersive hero carousel, followed by a benefits bar, clear product categories, highlighted featured products, problem/solution sections, compatibility badges, a dedicated music/audio focus, a FAQ section, and a comprehensive footer.
- Smooth and engaging micro-interactions are crucial, including buttery scroll behavior, intuitive swipe functionality for the hero carousel, and captivating animations for the simulated 3D product models. Product interactions will feature a floating effect, slow auto-rotation, subtle vertical movement, interactive swipe-to-rotate with inertia, and a gentle zoom on touch/hover, with seamless transitions to detail views.
